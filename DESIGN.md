# RYVA.ID Design System Direction

## Brand Identity & Soul
- **Brand**: RYVA.ID
- **Tagline**: Research to Reality
- **Philosophy**: Bringing research from experiments and prototypes to real-world industrial and societal implementation.
- **Tone**: Credible, innovative, scientifically grounded, pragmatic, and high-impact.

## Antislop Dials
- **ENERGY**: 2 (Balanced. Professional research organization with modern edge, avoiding both sleepy government portals and flashy crypto landing pages)
- **RHYTHM**: 2 (Structured variety. Purposeful section transitions: Hero -> Pipeline Flow -> Research Focus -> Case Studies -> Publications -> Collaboration CTA)
- **MOTION**: 2 (Purposeful transitions, smooth modal state, clean tab switching, no disorienting parallax or bounce loops)

## Color Palette (Strict 3-Color Hierarchy)
1. **Base Background**:
   - `bg-dark-root`: `#0A0D14` (Deep obsidian dark background)
   - `bg-dark-surface`: `#111622` (Clean card surface)
   - `bg-dark-elevated`: `#182232` (Header / modal surfaces)
2. **Text & Contrast (WCAG AA Compliant)**:
   - Primary Text: `#F8FAFC` (Slate-50, contrast ratio > 14:1)
   - Secondary Text: `#94A3B8` (Slate-400, contrast ratio > 5.5:1)
   - Muted Labels: `#64748B` (Slate-500, contrast ratio > 4.6:1 on surface)
3. **Primary Accent (Applied Science & Innovation)**:
   - Emerald / Teal: `#10B981` / `#059669` (Symbolizing sustainable growth and tangible real-world results)
4. **Secondary Precision Accent (Digital & AI Intelligence)**:
   - Cyan: `#06B6D4` / `#0891B2` (Used sparingly for technical data pills and active states)

## Typography & Hierarchy
- **Primary Typeface**: Inter, system-ui, sans-serif
- **Headings**: Sharp weight, deliberate tracking, no exaggerated letter-spacing.
- **Readable Prose**: Line-height 1.7, maximum reading width 68ch for article detail.

## Antislop Hard Gates Adherence
- Zero em dashes (`—`) in UI copy.
- Zero fake metrics or fabricated testimonials.
- Complete functional interactive controls (no dead links, no broken dropdowns).
- Full responsiveness on mobile (minimum 44px tap targets, zero horizontal overflow).
- All interactive controls accessible via keyboard (`Tab`, `Enter`, `Escape`).
